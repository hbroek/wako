# Attachments

The media library also keeps **PDF files**. Upload one, or drop it on the editor, and **Image / PDF…** inserts a link.

- [Download the demo handbook](handbook.pdf)

A PDF is stored exactly as uploaded: wako checks its type and size, does not read or change it, and does not show it inside the page. It travels in exports and imports like pictures do.

## Print this wiki as a PDF

The **PDF** button in the top bar opens a print view of a page and the pages below it, or of the whole space. Choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog.
