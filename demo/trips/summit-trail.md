# {{page.title}}

A day hike to the summit and back. **{{page.distance}}** return, **{{page.climb}}** of climbing.

![Map of the trail from the trailhead to the summit](../trail-map.svg)

## Plan

1. Start at the trailhead before 8:00
2. Follow the ridge for about two hours
3. Eat at the summit, then come down the same way
