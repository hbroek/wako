# Links

## Links inside the wiki

A link to another page is plain Markdown, written relative to this page:

- A page next to this one: [Tables](tables)
- A page below another page: [Summit trail](trips/summit-trail)
- With a heading: [the kanban section of the board](board)
- Back to the [welcome page](welcome)

When you rename or move a page, wako rewrites the links that pointed at it. Open **Backlinks** on [Tables](tables) to see this page listed.

A link to a page that does not exist yet shows in red and leads to a "create this page" form: [a page nobody has written](future-idea).

## Links to the outside

- A normal link: [Go](https://go.dev/)
- A link with a title: [SQLite](https://www.sqlite.org/ "A small, fast SQL database")
- A bare address becomes a link by itself: https://commonmark.org/
- The project: [wako on GitHub](https://github.com/hbroek/wako)
